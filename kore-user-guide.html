About the Portal

What Kore Kollective does and how the work flows

💡

Overview

Kore Kollective is a footwear distribution portal. It manages purchasing from vendors, warehouse stock, distributor orders, dispatch, returns and payments in one system, so that every team works from the same up-to-date records.

Signing in and understanding the six roles

1
Open the portalUse the portal link in any web browser.

Enter your credentialsUse the email address and password provided to you by the Super Admin.
3
Select LoginYou will be taken to the dashboard for your role.

Roles at a Glance

🛡️

Super Admin Full access

  • Full control of the portal, including user accounts, notifications and integrations.
👑

Admin High access

  • Runs the business: purchasing, orders, bills, payments and distributors. Cannot create user accounts.
🗂️

Manager Operations

  • Works across purchasing, stock, sales and distributors. Views purchase orders, orders and returns; cannot approve or change them.
🔍

Supervisor Operations

  • Works at warehouse level: receives goods through GRN and monitors stock and orders.
💰

Accountant Finance

  • Works in Accounts: vendor bills and overdue payments. Also views orders and reports.
🏪

Distributor Customer

  • Browses the catalogue, places orders and tracks them. Sees only their own data.

Typical Tasks

1
Order goods from a vendorPurchases → PO → create a new PO, select the vendor, add items and save as Draft or send it.
2
Receive goodsPurchases → GRN → select the PO and scan each carton. Stock is updated when the GRN is submitted.
3
Process a distributor orderSales → Orders → open the order, confirm it, then dispatch it with the carton scan and transport details.
4
Approve a vendor billAccounts → Bills → open the bill and approve or reject it.
5
Record a paymentAccounts → Overdue Payments, or Sales → Orders → mark the order as paid.
6
Create a user accountUsers → add the name, email, password and role.

Overdue Payments

Each distributor has agreed payment terms (30 days by default). An order becomes overdue when payment has not been received within those terms after delivery. Overdue orders appear in yellow; those more than 30 days past due appear in red.

Distributor

Placing and tracking orders

🏪

Your Portal

Distributors see a dedicated portal with the Dashboard, Catalogue, My Cart, Order History and Profile. Purchasing, vendor and accounting areas are not shown.

How to Place an Order

1
Open the CatalogueBrowse the products currently available.
2
Select a product and enter the quantityOrders are placed in cartons. One carton contains 24 pairs in a fixed size assortment.
3
Select Add to CartYou can add several products to the same order.
4
Open My Cart and reviewCheck quantities, choose the applicable GST rate and review the total. Your agreed discount is applied automatically.
5
Place the orderOrders are checked against your available credit before they are accepted.
6
Track the orderOpen Order History to follow progress and download documents.

Order Status

Pending
Order received
Dispatched
Goods shipped
In Transit
On the way
Delivered
Goods received

Pre-Orders

  • Products marked “Available in 30 Days” can be pre-booked.
  • Pre-orders are not checked against your credit limit.
  • Status shows as Pre-Booked, then Confirmed.

Changing an Order

  • An order can be edited while it is Pending or Pre-Booked.
  • It can be cancelled until it is dispatched.
  • On delivery, enter the receiver’s name and mobile number and attach the receiving note.

Credit Limit

  • The credit limit is the maximum order value allowed at one time.
  • If an order exceeds your available credit, it is not accepted.
  • Credit becomes available again once payments are received.

Documents

  • Proforma invoice (PDF or Excel)
  • Tax invoice
  • E-way bill and transport bill

Important Rules

Conditions the portal applies automatically

1
One carton equals 24 pairs

Distributor orders are placed in whole cartons. Each carton holds a fixed assortment of sizes.

2
Credit limit is checked on every order

An order that exceeds the distributor’s available credit is not accepted. Pre-orders are exempt.

3
Stock is updated through GRN

Received goods are added to stock only when the GRN has been submitted.

4
Discount and GST are applied automatically

Each distributor’s discount is set by the Admin and applied at checkout. GST is selected in the cart.

5
Payment terms decide when a payment is overdue

Terms are set for each distributor. The Accounts section lists every order that has passed its due date.

6
Pre-orders

Products not yet in stock are shown as “Available in 30 Days” and can be pre-booked before they arrive.

7
Every action is recorded

The Activity Log keeps a record of who did what and when. It is available to the Super Admin and Admin.
